# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Treat contributors, users and maintainers with respect. Discuss technical choices
using evidence, explain disagreements clearly, accept corrections and focus
criticism on the work. Help people with different backgrounds participate.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
and publishing another person's private information without consent are not
acceptable. Do not use project spaces for spam or repeated unwanted contact.

## Scope

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project
spaces, as well as public interactions where someone represents BindWitness.
They apply equally to users, contributors and maintainers.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainer at
[zheltov_pe@mail.ru](mailto:zheltov_pe@mail.ru). Include relevant links, what
happened and any immediate concern. Do not post sensitive details publicly.
Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will review the available context, seek clarification when needed
and limit sharing of private reports to what is necessary to address them.
Depending on severity and repetition, responses can include requesting a change
in behavior, removing content, or temporarily or permanently restricting
participation. A person affected by a decision may request reconsideration
through the same private contact, with additional relevant context.
